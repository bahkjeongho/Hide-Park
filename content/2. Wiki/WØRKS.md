> [!Info]
> WØRKS specializes in culturally-driven, visually refined work across _strategy, visual identity, creative direction_ and _design._  
> 
  Our name is an ode to the _Deutscher Werkbund_, the early 20th-century German collective that inspired the _Bauhaus_ and championed holistic design with the motto: _VOM SOFAKISSEN ZUM STÄDTEBAU_ (from sofa cushions to city planning). As such, we work _across mediums_, starting from _cultural context_ and _strategic clarity_ to create work that is both _elevated and inevitable_.
