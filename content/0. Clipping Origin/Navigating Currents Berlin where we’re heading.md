Currents Berlin is right around the corner, running from September 10–12, 2026. This brand-new, decentralized design festival is focused on furniture, interior, and product design. With over 60 locations and participants across the city, it’s almost impossible to see it all. That’s why we’ve put together a list of our top go-to spots and events. Disclaimer: This list is definitely a bit personal and biased, considering how many of the exhibitors and designers we know personally. <3

## Seetech by Weißensee Academy of Art Berlin

If you want to support emerging talent during the festival, the premiere of seetech x currents should be at the top of your list. For the first time, the Weißensee Academy of Art Berlin is launching its new _seetech_ label, showcasing exemplary work from its prototyping labs and alumni to highlight how academic research evolves into real-world design.

More info [here](https://www.currentsberlin.com/program/seetech10 "seetech weißensee exhibition").

## Einfach gut – Udk & Friends

Another highlight for student work is a joint exhibition between UdK Berlin’s Product Design program, HfG Karlsruhe, and HBK Saar. Students from all three universities will showcase a mix of semester projects and thesis work.

More info [here](https://www.currentsberlin.com/program/einfach-gut-udk-friends-thu "UDK exhibition").

## (Behälter) x Magazin

This exhibition explores the very origins of design through one of humanity’s fundamental need: storage. Conceived by Martina Dimitrova, the exhibition brings together well-loved storage objects, and their everyday contents, pulled directly from the personal lives of local architects, artists, craftspeople, and designers. (Full disclosure: the thing Magazine founder Anton Rahlwes contributed one of the featured exhibits.)

More info [here](https://www.currentsberlin.com/program/behalter "behälter exhibition").

## Tasche.Tüte.Tote

Conceived by designers Luisa Kahlfeldt and Christina Looshorn in collaboration with office brand Lintex, this exhibition showcases bag designs by Berlin-based creatives. Looking beyond the finished product, the show explores the creative process behind each piece.

With works of Christina Looshorn, Geckler Michels, JSR Studio, Lea Roesch, Obscure Objects, Simon Stanislawski, Sac Objects, Soft Divider, Tim Schütze, Rayas Collective.

More info [here](https://www.currentsberlin.com/program/taschetutetote-thu "taschen exhibition").

## Urban Tissue

Artist and architect Thilo Reich presents Urban Tissue, a sculptural exhibition exploring time, identity, and material memory at the Bundesstiftung Bauakademie. Running concurrently with Berlin Art Week and Currents Berlin, the show transforms urban surfaces and raw materials into poetic reflections on impermanence and human presence.

- When: September 10–13, 2026, 11:00 AM – 7:00 PM daily
    
- Where: Bundesstiftung Bauakademie (Red Hall & outdoor grounds), Schinkelplatz 1, 10117 Berlin
    

## Jalousiefabrik

Short on time? Head straight to the Jalousiefabrik in Berlin-Kreuzberg, a former window blinds factory hosting 14 perspectives from local brands and independent designers.

Featured Exhibitors: Balzer Balzer Studio, Baugruppe Jalousiefabrik, Celeste Burlina, Crafting Plastics, Find Attached, HAP Ceramics, Hot Legs, Laura Waclawek, Llot Llov, Moss Objects, Nils Sorge, Obscure Objects, Studio Mad, Tecta & Pascal Hien, Weberei

To explore related events and individual schedules, search for Jalousiefabrik on the [Currents Berlin program page](https://www.currentsberlin.com/program/).

Our selection is just a small slice of the massive program local designers and brands have put together for Currents Berlin. To explore everything happening across the city, check out the full lineup [on their website](https://www.currentsberlin.com/program/ "currents berlin program").

## Book launch and talk: Dialog Beyond Disciplines

Design duo Corinna Gratzl and Julia Klass (In Search Of) and Verlag Form host the launch of the book “Dialog Beyond Disciplines,” paired with a panel discussion on open-disciplinary ways of thinking and working in contemporary design. Hosted at communication agency Bureau N (which is also supporting Currents with public relations), the talk brings together the book's authors alongside several featured contributors.

Panelists: Elena Schütz (Something Fantastic), Judith Haase (Gonzalez Haase AAS), Lukas Marstaller (BNAG), Sarah Meyers & Laura Fügmann, and Corinna Gratzl & Julia Klass (In Search Of).

More info [here](https://www.currentsberlin.com/program/book-launch-and-talk-dialog-beyond-disciplines "book launch beyond disciplines").
